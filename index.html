About
Sheng-Wei Wang (Member, IEEE) received the B.S. degree in information management from National Taiwan University, Taipei, Taiwan, in 2001, and the Ph.D. degree in communications engineering from the National Tsing Hua University, Hsinchu, Taiwan, in 2008.,After one-year military service, he has been a Faculty Member with the Department of Applied Informatics, Fo Guang University, Yilan, Taiwan, from 2009. He was a Faculty Member with the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, Tamkang University, Taipei, from 2021 to 2022. Since August 2022, he has been a Faculty Member with the Department of Electronic Engineering, National United University, Miaoli, Taiwan, where he is currently an Associate Professor. His research interests include block chain, routing, and wavelength assignment (RWA) algorithms in all-optical WDM networks, peer-to-peer applications, queueing theory, and performance evaluation.(
